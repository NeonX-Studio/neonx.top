Light
Light City

Light is a systemic narrative RPG prototype set in Light City, an underground civilization shaped by scarcity, memory, and the pressure of a world that must keep moving. The player enters a city where every layer carries its own rhythms, needs, and unanswered questions.

Back to projects ↗
Core direction
A living underground city, system-driven narrative, and consequences that carry forward.
AI boundary
AI proposes context-aware dialogue and tasks while rules and world state remain authoritative.
Technology
Unreal Engine 5.8.3, C++, structured world state, and data-driven systems.

Systems before plot.
Consequences before justice.

Light places language models inside the simulation?s reasoning layer. They receive only the context they need and propose dialogue, tasks, or events; the runtime decides what can enter the world.

The result is a world that can respond without losing its shape: authored rules remain visible, while small choices can gather weight over time.
