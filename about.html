About
NeonX.

An independent creative studio at the intersection of games and artificial intelligence.

Make systems
worth entering.

NeonX Studio explores how games, artificial intelligence, and authored worlds can shape one another. The studio’s work starts with playable questions: what should a system remember, what should it be allowed to do, and how can a player feel the consequences?

We publish a focused record of projects and ideas as they become ready to share. The public work begins with Light, an AI-native systemic narrative RPG prototype.

Games
Interactive worlds, systemic storytelling, and original creative direction.
Artificial intelligence
Bounded tools and reasoning layers that remain accountable to game rules.
