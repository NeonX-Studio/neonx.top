POSITIONING / STUDIO

Why NeonX Studio exists

NeonX Studio is an independent creative studio at the intersection of games and artificial intelligence. We are drawn to the space where authored intent meets systems that can respond, remember, and surprise.

That means beginning with a playable question, deciding which rules matter, and letting technology serve the feeling of the experience. The point is not to place AI on top of a game. It is to make the relationship between player, world, and system worth exploring.

PROJECT / LIGHT

Building Light as a bounded AI world

Light is a systemic narrative RPG prototype set in Light City, an underground civilization shaped by pressure and possibility. Its central design question is how a world can feel alive without giving up authorship or reliable rules.

In the public project direction, AI acts as a bounded reasoning layer for dialogue, tasks, and event proposals. Gameplay rules and world state remain authoritative, keeping the system legible while leaving room for situations that feel discovered rather than scripted.
